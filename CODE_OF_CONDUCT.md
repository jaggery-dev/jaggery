# Code of conduct

Jaggery is made for households, by people who look after the computers for everyone else. We want working on it to feel the same way: patient, kind, and useful.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention, in any space connected to the project.
- Questions about "obvious" things are welcome. Everyone starts somewhere.

If something happens that shouldn't, email **hello@jaggery.dev**. Reports are read by a person and kept confidential. The maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions, or block people, who break these rules.
